# Bio.
Jiancheng (JC) Yang is a Principal Investigator at the [ELLIS Institute Finland](https://www.ellisinstitute.fi/) and Assistant Professor (Tenure-Track) at [Aalto University](https://www.aalto.fi/en), where he leads the Health Intelligence Lab. He received his Bachelor's and PhD degrees from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, was a [visiting fellow at Harvard](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=VWX-GMAAAAAJ), and completed [postdoctoral training at EPFL](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=kzFmAkYAAAAJ).

His research advances AI for health through generative and agentic AI, multimodal learning, and world models, with the goal of building virtual patients. JC has authored 80+ publications and is recognized for developing [MedMNIST](https://medmnist.com/). He serves as Area Chair for MICCAI/NeurIPS/MIDL and is an Editorial Board Member of npj Digital Medicine. He was the co-founder and CTO of [a medical AI startup](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/dianei), with NMPA-approved (China's FDA) products and multi-million-dollar venture funding. He is a [Forbes 30 Under 30](https://www.forbes.com/profile/yang-jiancheng/) honoree and a recipient of the [WAIC Yunfan Award](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/Lecza9L8zgZYDPdKpkP2XA).

As a personal interest, JC is the initiator and organizer of the HIT Webinar ([hit-webinar.com](https://hit-webinar.github.io/)), a Chinese-language webinar series focusing on healthcare, artificial intelligence, and cutting-edge technology. This fully open, non-profit initiative has hosted 100+ sessions since its launch in April 2022.
